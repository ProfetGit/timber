**Chop one log and the whole tree comes down, cartoon style.**

A vanilla data pack for **Minecraft Java 26.2 and 26.3**. It runs on the server only, so players join with an unmodified game. It also comes as a mod (Fabric, Quilt, NeoForge, Forge) and a server plugin (Paper, Purpur, Spigot, Bukkit).

![Features](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfetGit/timber/main/docs/desc/title-features.png)

- **The whole tree falls, cartoon style.** It shivers, drops onto the stump, leans back with a creak, bends as it tips over, slams down and splats its crown, then pops apart log by log. Smooth at any frame rate.
- **One tree at a time.** In a crowded forest, neighbouring trees keep their own logs and leaves.
- **Never fells builds.** Logs you placed are left alone, and so is anything without a leafy crown.
- **Falls where it makes sense:** away from you, or into open space when something is in the way.
- **Everything attached comes along,** such as vines, cocoa, hanging moss and snow.
- **Every overworld tree,** including the 2×2 giants, and poplar on 26.3.
- **Fair durability.** The axe pays for the whole tree, Unbreaking included. A tree that would break your axe isn't felled.
- **Everyone chooses.** Any player can turn it off or on for themselves with `/trigger timber`.

![How to use](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfetGit/timber/main/docs/desc/title-how-to-use.png)

1. Hold an axe.
2. Chop a log of a natural tree.

Sneak to take a single log instead.

![Settings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfetGit/timber/main/docs/desc/title-settings.png)

Operators open a clickable menu with `/function timber:settings`. It sets the maximum tree size, how sneaking works, where drops land, whether an axe is needed, durability use, the animation and placed-log protection.

![Add-ons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfetGit/timber/main/docs/desc/title-add-ons.png)

**[Enchanted Timber](https://modrinth.com/datapack/enchanted-timber)** turns tree felling into an axe enchantment you have to find first. Making your own add-on? See the [add-on API](https://github.com/ProfetGit/timber/blob/main/docs/api.md).

![Installation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfetGit/timber/main/docs/desc/title-installation.png)

- **Data pack:** put the `.zip` in your world's `datapacks` folder and run `/reload`. Don't unzip it.
- **Mod:** put the `-fabric.jar` (Fabric or Quilt, needs Fabric API) or the `-forge.jar` (Forge or NeoForge) in `mods`.
- **Plugin:** put the `-plugin.jar` in `plugins` and restart the server.

Use only one of the three. To remove Timber cleanly, run `/function timber:uninstall` first.
